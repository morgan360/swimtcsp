# 🏊 Booking Public Swims

Booking a public swim at our facility is easy, fast, and secure. Here's everything you need to know:

---

## ✅ How Do I Book?

To book a public swim:

- Create an account using your **email** and **password** (this only needs to be done once).
- Log in and **book and pay online** for your chosen session.
- Spaces are limited — customers with a valid **email receipt** or **QR code** will be admitted first.

---

## 🏠 Can I Book at the Door?

Yes, **walk-in bookings** are accepted if places are still available.  
However, we strongly recommend **booking online** to:

- Guarantee your place
- Avoid delays at check-in  
Just show your **QR code** at the front desk when you arrive.

---

## 💳 What If My Card Isn’t Accepted?

We accept all major credit cards.  
If you're having issues at checkout, please **contact us directly** and we’ll help you complete your booking.

---

## 🔒 Is Online Payment Secure?

Yes — your payment is processed through **Bank of Ireland’s secure payment system**.  
We do **not store** any of your confidential card information.

---

## 📅 Where Can I See Swim Times?

You can check swim availability in two ways:

- Use the **“Public Swims Booking”** link in the main menu
- Visit the **“Public Swims”** tab in our [timetable](#) section
