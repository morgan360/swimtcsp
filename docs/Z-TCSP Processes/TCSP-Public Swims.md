# 🏊 Booking Public Swims

Booking a public swim is easy and secure. Here's how it works:

## ✅ How do I book?
To book a public swim:

- Create an account with your **email** and **password** (this only needs to be done once).
- Once logged in, you can **book and pay online** for available sessions.
- Spaces are limited. Customers with valid **email receipts** or **QR codes** will be admitted first.

## 🏠 Can I book at the door?
Yes, **walk-in bookings** are allowed if places are still available.  
However, we strongly recommend **booking online** to guarantee your spot and avoid delays.  
Just show your **QR code** at the desk when you arrive.

## 💳 What if my card isn't accepted?
We accept all major credit cards.  
If you're having trouble, please **give us a call** and we’ll assist you directly.

## 🔒 Is online payment secure?
Absolutely.  
We use **Bank of Ireland’s secure payment system**, and we **do not store any confidential card details** on our servers.

## 📅 Where can I find session times?
You can:

- Use the **“Public Swims Booking”** option in the main menu, or  
- Check the **“Public Swims”** tab on our [timetable](#) page.
