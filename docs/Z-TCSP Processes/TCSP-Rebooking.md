## Rebooking

### Process

Rebooking starts before the term ends at the **rebooking_date** and ends at **booking_date**. It is exclusive to 
swimlings booked into the current term. Guardians can only book a swimling into the same class in the next term. 
They book each swimling lesson independently. During the rebooking phase, other guardians can still book into the 
current term. After **booking_date** before the end of term. Anyone can book into the next term(there will be no 
booking into current term)

### Programming

When the user clicks the Rebooking panel they are brought directly to the shopping_cart app and the payment page 
**direct_rebooking.html** and if the click the button **Confirm and Pay** they are brought to the 
**confirm_rebooking** view which directs them to BOIPA payment.
