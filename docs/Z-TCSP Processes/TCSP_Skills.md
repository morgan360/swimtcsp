## Skill Stages

Skills are divided into **8 stages** which encompass both widths and lengths classes. The stages are designed to mark moments of progress and highlight areas that need improvement. Stages, while based off existing class levels, are no longer strictly linear, meaning teachers can teach and grade skills regardless of what level they teach.

The aim of these stages is to shift the focus from where in the pool a child is swimming to the skills they are developing.

Children's lessons and skills are related as follows:

    * Beginners 1: Stage 1
    * Beginners 2: Stage 2
    * Improvers 1: Stage 3
    * Improvers 2: Stage 4
    * Advanced: Stage 5
    * Lengths Level 1: Stage 6
    * Lengths Level 2: Stage 7
    * Lengths Level 3: Stage 8
