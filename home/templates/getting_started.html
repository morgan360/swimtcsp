Getting Started

Two quick ways to get in the water

Welcome! Start your swimming journey with us – whether you're looking for lessons or just want to enjoy public swims, we have something for everyone. Simply create a free account to get started! Keep reading for a quick guide on how to get started.

Book in for Lessons

Adult or child, beginner or advanced, all term-based lessons are managed through the swimling dashboard. Follow these steps to get started:

  1. Create an account and check the 'I wish to sign up for swimming lessons.'. This makes you a Guardian, meaning you can register yourself or others for lessons in the swimling dashboard.
    • If you already have an account but do not have Guardian permissions, please visit your profile to become a guardian.
    • If you already have Guardian permissions, please visit the swimling dashboard to register a swimling and book classes.

  2. Visit the swimling dashboard and register a swimling by clicking the 'Add New Swimling' button and filling out their details.

  3. You can now book lessons, rebook for the next term, join the waiting list, and edit your swimling's details all in one place!

You can access your swimling dashboard through 'Main Menu' in the navigation, or through your profile dropdown in the top-right corner of the page.

Schools Lessons

To register for schools classes, please follow the above steps. You must be a guardian and have registered at least one swimling.

  1. Go to your profile and click 'Join schools' in the quick actions section. Follow the instructions.
  2. Make sure the swimling you want to register has the correct school role number. You can check this by editing their information in the Swimling Dashboard.
  3. Once your swimling has the correct school role number, the schools panel should become visible in the Swimling Dashboard. If you cannot see it, please contact us.

Book in for Public Swims

Join our open swim sessions for fitness or fun. All our public swims are pay-as-you-go. Booking is easy:


  1. Create an account. Only check 'I wish to sign up for swimming lessons.' if you wish to register for lessons too. If you're unsure, you can always contact us or add this feature in your profile later.

  2. Browse our selection of public swims here to find a swim session that fits your schedule.

  3. Follow the prompts to book your session.
