Templeogue College Swim Pool

For the community

Here at Templeogue College Swim Pool we have a five lane 25m heated pool (30°C), which has two shallow ends of 3’ and 3’3” in depth. This gives us the benefit of extra shallow space and is perfect for beginners and those learning to swim. A deep section in the middle of the pool enables deep water development. So whether you are joining us for public swims, lessons, training, development or fitness, along with our highly trained and experienced staff we have your aquatic needs covered.

To use our services all you need to do is sign up. It's simple and free - all you need is your email address.

Our Services

Explore our range of services designed for all ages and skill levels.

Pool Timetable

Children's Swimming Lessons

Our Learn to Swim program takes your child from their first experience in the water to confident, fitness-focused swimming. Starting with Beginners, children build confidence and essential water skills. As they progress through Improvers and Advanced levels, they refine technique, develop stamina, and gain deepwater experience. In the final stages—Lengths Levels 1 to 3—the focus shifts to swimming for fitness, stroke refinement, and endurance. Each level builds on the last, helping swimmers grow in ability, confidence, and enjoyment of the water.

Unsure what level would suit your child best? Please contact us for guidance.

Adult's Beginners Lessons

Our adult swimming lessons support you from your first steps in the water through to confident, fitness-focused swimming. Whether you're a complete beginner or returning after time away, we have a space for you. This session is split into three progressive levels: the first for absolute beginners building confidence and safety skills, the second for developing technique and strength, and a lane-based level focused on increasing distance and endurance.

Each level is designed to help adults grow at their own pace, guided by experienced instructors in a supportive, inclusive environment.

Not sure which level is right for you? Please contact us for guidance.

Adults & Teens

Ideal for swimmers progressing from our beginner classes or those seeking distance without the intensity of Masters sessions, these coached lessons focus on improving fitness in a supportive, structured environment. Suitable for both adults and teens.

Masters Swims

Our coached lane swims are designed for swimmers aged 16+ who are confident swimming lengths and want to take their training to the next level. These sessions focus on building endurance, improving technique, and increasing overall intensity—perfect for those training for competitions, open water events, or triathlons. Led by experienced coaches, each session offers structured sets aimed at enhancing performance over distance. Held every Monday and Wednesday, with convenient pay-as-you-go access.

Aqua Aerobics

Dive into fitness with our Aqua Aerobics classes - fun, energetic, and set to music! Suitable for all ages and genders, these sessions are perfect for burning calories, improving overall fitness, and toning muscles in a supportive, low-impact environment.

Ladies Swims

We offer a selection of ladies-only swim sessions, including coached lane swims on Wednesday mornings and Fridays for those seeking distance and structure. For those new to swimming or building confidence, our ladies-only beginners class provides the perfect place to start or continue your swimming journey.

Public Swims

Our public swim sessions are open to everyone in the community - whether you're coming for a relaxing solo dip, bringing the whole family, or enjoying some time in the water with friends. We welcome individuals, families, senior citizens, and ladies. Some sessions also include designated lanes for length swimming. Public swims are available throughout the week, including weekdays, evenings, and weekend afternoons.
