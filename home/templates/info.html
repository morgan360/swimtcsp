Templeogue College Swimming Pool

Here at Templeogue College Swim Pool we have a five lane 25m heated pool, which has two shallow ends of 3’ and 3’3” in depth. This gives us the benefit of extra shallow space and is perfect for beginners and those learning to swim. A deep section in the middle of the pool enables deep water development. So whether you are joining us for public swims, lessons, training, development or fitness, along with our highly trained and experienced staff we have your aquatic needs covered.

To use our services all you need to do is Register. It is simple and free. All you need is your email address.
(If you wish also to register for swimming lessons or our schools program, tick the appropriate box or change it later under your profile.)
