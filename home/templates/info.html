Templeogue College Swim Pool

For the community

Here at Templeogue College Swim Pool we have a five lane 25m heated pool (30°C), which has two shallow ends of 3' and 3'3" in depth. This gives us the benefit of extra shallow space and is perfect for beginners and those learning to swim. A deep section in the middle of the pool enables deep water development. So whether you are joining us for public swims, lessons, training, development or fitness, along with our highly trained and experienced staff we have your aquatic needs covered.

To use our services all you need to do is sign up. It's simple and free - all you need is your email address.

Our Services

Explore our range of services designed for all ages and skill levels

Children's Swimming Lessons

Our Learn to Swim program takes your child from their first experience in the water to confident, fitness-focused swimming. Starting with Beginners, children build confidence and essential water skills.

Unsure what level would suit your child best? Please contact us for guidance.

Get started by registering a swimling in the Swimling Dashboard.

Adult's Beginners Lessons

Our adult swimming lessons support you from your first steps in the water through to confident, fitness-focused swimming. Whether you're a complete beginner or returning after time away, we have a space for you.

Not sure which level is right for you? Please contact us for guidance.

Coached Lanes (13+)

A lanes session ideal for those progressing from children's lengths, teens continuing to swim, adults progressing from widths/beginners lessons, and beginner triathletes.

Masters Swims

Our coached lane swims are designed for swimmers aged 16+ who are confident swimming lengths and want to take their training to the next level. Perfect for those training for competitions, open water events, or triathlons.

Aqua Aerobics

Dive into fitness with our Aqua Aerobics classes - fun, energetic, and set to music! Suitable for all ages and genders, these sessions are perfect for burning calories and improving overall fitness.

Ladies Swims

We offer a selection of ladies-only swim sessions, including coached lane swims and beginners classes. The perfect place to start or continue your swimming journey in a comfortable environment.

Public Swims

Our public swim sessions are open to everyone in the community - whether you're coming for a relaxing solo dip, bringing the whole family, or enjoying some time in the water with friends.

TeamTSC

We are also home to TeamTSC, our competitive swimming team. While we do not deal with club inquiries directly, you can find out more by visiting their website .
