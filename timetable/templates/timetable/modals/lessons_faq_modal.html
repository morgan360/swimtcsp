Swimming Lessons FAQs

We offer lessons to children four years old and above.

You can book term-based lessons through your swimling dashboard. Add a swimling, click 'book', and simply complete the booking and payment process. You can register your interest in joining classes that are currently fully booked. Rebooking is also done through the dashboard.

Please bring appropriate swimwear, a towel, a hat, and goggles. For children in Beginners, please bring armbands if necessary. We provide all teaching aids and equipment.

Our lesson terms run for 3 months, so each term varies slightly in length. Our lessons run year-round. Terms start in January, April, July, and October.

Unfortunately, we cannot offer refunds for lessons missed due to holidays or other personal commitments. For the Summer term (July - Sept) there is a coupon which can be used if you will miss 2 weeks for holidays. In the case of illness, medical credit can be allocated on a case by case basis. Medical credit can be issued for long term absence only with a doctor's letter.

No, our teachers do not teach scripted lessons.

We currently do not offer private or 1-2-1 lessons.

Progression of levels is decided by the class teacher. We will notify you when your child is ready to move. If you require information on your child's progression please speak to their class teacher.

Swim shoes are not allowed during swimming lessons. Swim socks are only recommended if required for a current foot complaint. Flip-flops/crocs are sufficient foot hygiene travelling to and from the pool area.
