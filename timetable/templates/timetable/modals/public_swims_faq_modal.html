Public Swims FAQs

You can book public swim sessions through our booking system. Simply select your preferred time slot, choose the number of swimmers, and complete the payment process. You can also pay at reception.

All public sessions will have a lifeguard on duty.

Our public swim sessions welcome swimmers of all ages. We have family-friendly sessions, as well as some specialized sessions for ladies only. Children under 8 must be accompanied by an adult at all times.

We have a 5-lane 25m heated pool (30°C) with two shallow ends (3' and 3'3") and a deeper middle section. Changing rooms, lockers, and poolside seating are available. Some sessions include designated lanes for length swimming. We do not have a sauna, jacuzzi, or steam room.

Please bring appropriate swimwear, a towel, and any personal items you need. Goggles are optional but recommended for comfortable swimming. We provide lockers, but you may want to bring a small padlock or coins for the lockers.

We do not recommend the use of swim shoes/socks. Aqua shoes/socks can be worn during aqua aerobics classes.

Swim shoes are not allowed during swimming lessons. Swim socks are only recommended if required for a current foot complaint. Flip-flops/crocs are sufficient foot hygiene travelling to and from the pool area.
